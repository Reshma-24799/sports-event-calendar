
During the development of this Sports Events Calendar, I utilized AI tools primarily as a research 
and brainstorming assistant rather than as a complete code generator. My goal was to maintain full 
ownership of the logic and architecture while leveraging AI to speed up the development and learning process.

Specifically, I used AI to:
- Research best practices for handling and formatting dates in TypeScript, 
    especially regarding the complexities of rendering a calendar grid,
    how to add empty space so that date 1 begins under correct day.

- Brainstorm styling approaches to make the UI look clean and responsive using Tailwind CSS.
